FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you request a quote.

What file formats can I upload?

STL, STEP, OBJ, PDF, PNG, JPG and AI. If your file is very large, share a cloud-storage link in the notes.

How do I get a price?

Use the Get a Quote page. Tell us the service, material, colour, quantity and deadline, and upload your file. We reply with a quote.

How long does an order take?

It depends on the item, material and quantity. Add your deadline to the request and we will confirm what is possible.

Can you help me choose a material?

Yes. Describe what the part needs to do and we will recommend a material. See the material guide on the 3D Printing page.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Use the Track Order page with your quote or order reference.

How do I pay and how is my order delivered?

We confirm payment and delivery options with your quote. Contact us if you have a preference.

Still have a question?

Message us on WhatsApp or send a quote request.
